Privacy Policy – Units Guard

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Units Guard: Bijli Tracker (package com.muneerhasan.bijlitracker) is developed by Muneer Hasan. It helps households in Pakistan track their electricity meter readings and stay under 200 units a month. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. The app has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Your electricity company, meter readings, reading dates, billing month details and app settings are stored only in a local database on your phone.
  • This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup (see section 4).
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, permanently deletes all of it.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera (optional): used only when you tap “Scan meter” to read the number on your electricity meter. The image is processed on your phone using Google ML Kit on-device text recognition. Photos are not uploaded anywhere. A cropped image of the meter display is kept in the app’s private storage alongside that reading and is deleted when you delete the reading or uninstall the app. You can always type the reading instead and never grant camera access.
  • Notifications (optional): used to remind you to take a meter reading and to warn you when your usage is close to 200 units. These notifications are created on your phone; nothing is sent to or from a server. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings or deny the permission.

4. Backups

In Settings you can create a backup file of your readings and share it using any app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Google Drive), and later restore it. This only happens when you do it yourself. Once you share the file, it is handled by the app or service you chose, under their privacy policy.

5. Internet use

The app works fully offline and does not send your data over the internet. The only time it opens an internet page is if you tap the link to the official NEPRA website, which opens in your web browser.

6. Children

The app is a general household utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Not affiliated with any government or electricity company

Units Guard is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Government of Pakistan, NEPRA, WAPDA, or any electricity distribution company (LESCO, IESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO, TESCO, K-Electric). All unit forecasts and bill amounts are estimates for guidance only; your official bill is final.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes (for example if advertising is added in a future version), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the “Last updated” date will change.
